Sidebar artwork — Viper fork

These owner-selected marks decorate Elias's Viper interface. They do not imply
partnership or endorsement. Names, trademarks and artwork remain their respective
owners' property; the LobeHub vector's MIT notice below is not a trademark grant
or a blanket license for the other artwork.

Source/preparation records (retrieved 2026-11-03/04) were retained in the external
Viper-Interface project's assets/sidebar-art/ and new-logo-assets/ directories:
- Viper and Gentoo: images supplied by Elias; the selected PNG and SVG are unchanged.
- Ouroboros: complete https://ouroboros-agent.ai/assets/icon_1024.png, unchanged;
  only the displayed outer corners are rounded by CSS.
- OpenAI: native Blossom diagrams at https://openai.com/brand/; the first Blossom
  path is extracted without altering its geometry, with native black/white fills.
- Claude: https://assets-proxy.anthropic.com/claude-ai/v2/assets/v1/cd02a42d9-Vq_H3mgS.svg,
  the unchanged icon referenced by https://claude.ai/.
- Zed: https://zed.dev/brand; the exact monogram path is extracted from the
  wordmark canvas with the brand's permitted black/white treatment.
- Antigravity: https://antigravity.google/assets/image/brand/antigravity-icon__full-color.svg,
  the unchanged full-color mark listed at https://antigravity.google/press.
- Podman: https://podman.io/logos/optimized/podman-3-logo-266w-253h.webp,
  the unchanged filled emblem used by podman.io's navbar. LF Projects trademark
  terms remain separate from copying the image.
- Gemini: unchanged packages/static-svg/icons/gemini-color.svg from LobeHub's
  lobe-icons commit ea434b96e75813517b377825e01c0df61dee8836.
